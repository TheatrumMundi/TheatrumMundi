I would not attribute “everything” <br>
To “malice,” what little of it is <br>
Availed of truly who can say and hasn’t, <br>
More and less than what he wanted? <br>

I have said as much and as little as <br>
My small pity has allowed me to feel, <br>
For my part. You will know more <br>
Than I do, were I more honest. <br>
