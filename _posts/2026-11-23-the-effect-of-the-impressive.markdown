When nothing else is left, the impressive remains as the effect of capability without regard to end or quality. The superfluity of action is also the end of judgement, with action being the sieve through which the sensibility is refined towards the effects of man’s labours. I recognise that, even now, there are countless stupidities that are transparent to us simply because certain regular actions and resistances are no longer necessary. It remains to be seen whether even the effect of the good remains in the superfluity of action, without which continued life and suicide are indeterminate values distinguished only by one’s tolerance for pain and betrayal.
